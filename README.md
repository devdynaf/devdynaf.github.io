# DYNAF.GAMES

**Free browser games — no download, no account, just fun.**

 **Play now:** [devdynaf.github.io](https://devdynaf.github.io)

DYNAF.GAMES is a small, growing collection of browser-based games built with vanilla HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript. Every game runs instantly in your browser — desktop or mobile(although some ones may be platform specific), no installs, no sign-up, no ads getting in the way.

---

##  Games

###  [Redstone Lab](https://devdynaf.github.io/redstone-lab/)
A Minecraft-inspired redstone circuit simulator. Place wires, repeaters, pistons, and logic gates, then watch your circuits power up in real time. Perfect for anyone who loves logic puzzles, circuit design, or just blowing things up with redstone.

**Tags:** sandbox · logic builder · circuit simulator

###  [Falling Sand](https://devdynaf.github.io/falling-sand/)
A falling-sand physics sandbox. Simulate fire, water, oil, lava, acid, and more, and watch the elements react, burn, and flow in a persistent canvas.

**Tags:** physics sandbox · particle simulation · falling sand game

### [Clay 3D](https://devdynaf.github.io/clay-3d/)
A virtual pottery wheel that lets you squish, shape, and paint your own 3D ceramic models by clicking and dragging.

**Tags:** simulation · art · pottery

### [CYPHER](https://devdynaf.github.io/CYPHER/)
A virtual network hacking simulator, where you hack into networks to serve release files and serve justice.

**Tags:** simulation · hacking · computer

##  Why DYNAF.GAMES?

- **No account required** — open a link and start playing
- **No downloads** — everything runs in-browser via HTML5 Canvas/JS
- **Works on desktop and mobile**
- **Lightweight and fast-loading**, hosted on GitHub Pages

---

##  Tech Stack

- HTML5 / CSS3 / Vanilla JavaScript
- Canvas-based rendering for physics and grid simulations
- Hosted free on **GitHub Pages**
- No frameworks, no build step — just open and play
